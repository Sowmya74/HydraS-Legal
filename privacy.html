HydraS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HydraS, a hydration and drink-tracking app, handles information when you use the app.

1. Information You Enter

HydraS lets you record water intake, drink types, drink amounts, custom drink names, sugar intake, daily targets, and a display name. This information is used to provide the app's tracking and personalization features.

2. Storage of Information

HydraS may store information on your device to maintain your entries and preferences. The actual storage and synchronization behavior depends on the app's implementation and device settings. Please contact us if you have questions about how your information is stored.

3. Notifications

If you enable notifications, HydraS may send hydration reminders. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

This policy must accurately reflect any analytics, crash-reporting, cloud synchronization, or third-party services used by the app. Details about any information shared with those services should be provided here before the app is released.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain stored in the app until it is deleted through available app features or removed when the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Backups or synchronization, if enabled, may affect deletion behavior.

6. Children's Privacy

HydraS is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in ways that violate applicable privacy laws.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: hydras.support@gmail.com
